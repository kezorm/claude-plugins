# Journal

Append-only record of what was done and what was learned, **newest entry first.**

- **Current state** lives in [`README.md`](README.md) — that document is rewritten
  to stay true, and never carries history.
- **This file is never rewritten.** Past entries stand as written; when one turns
  out to be wrong, the correction goes in a *new* entry saying so.

Entries record what was filed, what was found, what it **corrected**, and what is
still open — including the dead ends, which are the most commonly omitted and
often the most useful part. One entry per thread of work, not per day.
